📝 Changelog: Sovereign-Resilience (v2.0)
Major Architectural & Brand Changes

Project Rebrand: Renamed application from "Sentinel Global Emergency Monitor" to "Sovereign-Resilience: Global Emergency Monitor".

Dual-Module Interface: Implemented a new Mother/Daughter toggle architecture, allowing seamless switching between the "GLOBAL THREAT MONITOR" and the new "HUMANITARIAN RESILIENCE" dashboard.

Updated Branding: Integrated the new Sovereign-Resilience red shield emblem and updated the global color palette to distinguish between Threat (Red) and Humanitarian (Green/Blue) statuses.

New Features: Humanitarian Resilience Module

Global Resilience Network: Added an interactive map specifically for tracking active humanitarian efforts and relief mobilization globally.

Live Relief Watch Feed: Created a dedicated intelligence feed for humanitarian updates, featuring quick-filter chips for Aid, Rescue, Reconstruction, Medical, Shelter, Food, and Water.

Global Aid Coordination Widget: Added live status tracking for major operational organizations, including the ICRC, WFP, MSF, UNHCR, and Red Cross.

Resilience Knowledge Base: Added actionable checklists for civilian survival, including "GRID DOWN / BLACKOUT PREP" protocols.

Active Countermeasures Panel: Introduced a sync panel linking specific global threats to tactical civilian solutions (e.g., Crisis Caregiving, Water Purification Basics).

Analog Resilience Protocols: Integrated a download portal for a comprehensive offline PDF resilience manual to ensure access to medical protocols and coordination maps during complete digital failure.

Humanitarian Readiness Meter: Added a distinct readiness level indicator specific to humanitarian operational status.

Enhancements: Global Threat Monitor & Core System

Intelligence Leader Watchlist: Added a visual tracking bar for key global leaders, complete with national flags (US, China, Russia, UK, France, Germany).

Critical Strategic Alerts: Introduced a high-visibility, swipeable broadcast panel for immediate strategic updates (e.g., NATO Defense Boosts).

Expanded Intelligence Filters: Upgraded the event filtering categories to comprehensively cover Warfare, Cyber Warfare, Economic Shocks, Finance, Stock Market, Agriculture, Space Weather, Terrorism, Seismic, Disasters, and Health.

System Integrity & Liability Footer: Added explicit creator credits ("LIBRA420T & ARIESSECRET3") and a mandatory liability disclaimer regarding the use of site data for emergency or financial planning.

Platform Definition Panel: Added a pop-up overlay detailing the Sovereign-Resilience protocol, data sources, and DEFCON level interpretations.
