## 🌐 Overview
**Sovereign-Resilience** is an advanced, dual-module monitoring platform designed to aggregate, analyze, and visualize global emergencies in real-time. Created by LIBRA420T & ARIESSECRET3, the platform serves as a unified command dashboard that tracks conflicts, natural disasters, and geopolitical tensions, while simultaneously monitoring global humanitarian relief efforts.


## 🏗️ The Mother/Daughter Architecture

The platform operates on a unified, toggleable interface that separates high-level threat intelligence from actionable humanitarian data.

### 🔴 GLOBAL THREAT MONITOR (The "Mother")
The primary command dashboard for situational awareness and threat detection:
* **System Readiness (DEFCON):** Real-time force readiness tracking (Levels 1-5).
* **Critical Strategic Alerts:** Live strategic broadcasts and immediate impact analysis on military and geopolitical movements.
* **Intelligence Leader Watchlist:** Tracks key global leaders and state actors relevant to ongoing crises.
* **Global Risk Mapping:** Real-time coordinate visualization of active intelligence nodes worldwide.
* **Active Emergency Feed:** Categorized threat monitoring across Warfare, Cyber Warfare, Economic Shocks, Finance, Space Weather, and Seismic events.

### 🌿 HUMANITARIAN RESILIENCE (The "Daughter")
A specialized sub-module providing deep-dive data on civilian impact, aid distribution, and analog survival:
* **Global Resilience Network:** Geospatial tracking of active humanitarian efforts and relief mobilization.
* **Live Relief Watch:** Filters real-time deployments by category: Aid, Rescue, Reconstruction, Medical, Shelter, Food, and Water.
* **Global Aid Coordination:** Operational status tracking for major NGOs including the ICRC, WFP, MSF, UNHCR, and Red Cross.
* **Resilience Knowledge & Active Countermeasures:** Step-by-step guides for Crisis Caregiving, Tactical Grounding, and Grid Down / Blackout Prep.
* **Analog Resilience Protocols:** Access to downloadable, offline PDF manuals detailing medical protocols, water purification, and local coordination for grid-down scenarios.


## 🛠️ Technical Stack
* **AI Analysis Engine:** Gemini Intelligence Network
* **Environment:** Google AI Studio
* **Deployment:** Google Cloud Run (Serverless)
* **Data Sources:** Cross-referenced global news feeds, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and official government/NGO broadcasts.
