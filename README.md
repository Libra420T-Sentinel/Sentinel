**Sovereign-Resilience Intelligence Network** is a state-of-the-art, full-stack intelligence platform designed for real-time monitoring of global geopolitical stability and humanitarian crises. By merging advanced data visualization with AI-driven analysis, it provides a comprehensive "Command and Control" interface for decision-makers, researchers, and global citizens.


## 🌐 The Vision

In an era of rapid geopolitical shifts and increasing natural disasters, information is the ultimate currency of resilience. This platform serves as a dual-perspective lens:
1. ‼️ **The Threat Perspective**: Monitoring military readiness, cyber anomalies, and geopolitical friction points.
2. 🌱 **The Resilience Perspective**: Tracking aid corridors, medical supplies, and humanitarian efforts that rebuild stability.


## 🚀 Key Features

### 📡 Real-Time Intelligence Feed
- **WebSocket Integration**: Live streaming of global anomalies and humanitarian updates.
- **Anomaly Detection**: Monitoring for cyber-attacks, economic shifts, and military movements.
- **Humanitarian Tracking**: Real-time status of medical, food, water, and rescue missions.

### 🛡️ Dynamic DEFCON System
- **Threat Level Assessment**: Automated and manual DEFCON level adjustments (1-5).
- **Visual/Auditory Alerts**: Immersive UI transitions and sound cues for critical status changes.
- **Panic Mode**: A high-intensity interface toggle for maximum focus during critical events.

### 🧠 AI-Powered Analysis (Gemini AI)
- **Stability Briefings**: Automated summaries of global stability using Google Gemini.
- **Source Verification**: AI-driven search for official reports (Reuters, AP, UN) to combat misinformation.
- **Geopolitical Context**: Deep-dive analysis into the "why" behind global shifts.

### 🗺️ Interactive Global Mapping
- **D3.js Visualizations**: High-fidelity map showing crisis hotspots and aid distribution.
- **Location Intelligence**: Search and filter intelligence by specific regions or countries.

### 📄 Professional Reporting
- **PDF Export**: Generate instant intelligence briefings and situation reports (SITREPs) for offline use.
- **History Logs**: Comprehensive audit trail of all status changes and critical alerts.

## 📖 Usage

- **Initial Scan**: Upon launch, initiate a "Global Scan" to populate the network with real-time data.
- **Toggle Modes**: Use the dashboard toggle to switch between **THREAT** (Red/Black) and **RESILIENCE** (Green/Black) perspectives.
- **Search**: Use the global search bar to focus on specific countries or crisis types.
- **Alerts**: Enable browser notifications to stay informed even when the tab is in the background.
